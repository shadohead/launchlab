Battle Pass tester

Talks to a LaunchLab stick's Battle Pass profile the way the Beyblade X app does, and shows what comes back.

Connection

Not connected

On the stick: Menu → Bluetooth, turned on. Menu → Bluetooth → Beyblade X shows the launches waiting; hold A there for the pass's button. Close the Beyblade X app first: the stick takes one connection at a time.

Commands

Clear deletes the launches waiting for the app, as a finished app sync does. Power off ends the session: the stick drops the link 300 ms later.
